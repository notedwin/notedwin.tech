## Six in, six out

Added:

- **Immich:** photo and video backup, like Google Photos, but the photos live on my machine
- **Beszel:** lightweight server monitoring
- **Memos:** quick notes
- **Healthchecks:** alerts when a scheduled job doesn't run
- **Glance:** a dashboard for feeds and widgets
- **Infisical:** secret management (though I'm still using .env files for now)

Removed: Motioneye, OpenWRT, Dagster, Grafana, Filestash and Homepage. They either added complexity I didn't need, or I wasn't using them enough to justify maintaining them.

## The rule

A homelab makes me better at trading off money, time and learning. This year that came down to two rules:

- Spending 20 hours to save $10 is a bad trade.
- Spending 40 hours on a project nobody will use regularly is just as bad.

Looking back, it's gone in three phases: learning (Linux, Docker, infrastructure as code), building (my personal data platform), and now cutting back to what I actually use.

## Plans

Do I want a small rack? Yes. Does a full Ubiquiti setup sound cool? Absolutely. Do I want to spend a fortune? Not really.

Next is a NAS for photos, videos and personal documents, probably TrueNAS running inside Proxmox, connected to my laptop over Thunderbolt for fast transfers.

## Looking back (2026)

"Not really" lasted a year. I moved and went all in on UniFi. The NAS happened differently: a prebuilt N5 running TrueNAS directly, with Proxmox on the GPU server instead. [Thunderbolt](/blog/thunderbolt) works, but I rarely need it. More in [2026](/blog/homelab-2026).
